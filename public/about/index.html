About Stateway

Building durable businesses with technology and research.

Stateway Capital Group Limited is a Tanzania-based group developing technology, business systems and data-driven research initiatives for African markets.

What we are building

Stateway brings together practical software, financial-intelligence technology and structured research under one group. The focus is on products and operating systems that can become useful infrastructure for businesses and decision-makers.

How the group is structured

Kraftech is Stateway’s technology and digital business systems brand. Stateway Terminal is the group’s financial intelligence and analytics platform. Each product has a distinct purpose while sharing a common technology and research foundation.
